Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about dining, seating reservations, weekend live music, party packages, and food delivery at Palate Story.

Palate Story is conveniently situated on the 2nd Floor, GS Road, right next to the Six Mile Flyover, Guwahati (PIN: 781022). The building features easy elevator and wide stair access. Convenient roadside parking as well as commercial building parking are available for our patrons.

We are open 7 days a week from 11:30 AM to 10:30 PM. Our kitchen takes last orders at 10:00 PM. Weekend live music sessions generally kick off around 8:00 PM on Friday and Saturday evenings.

Palate Story specializes in a curated tri-cuisine menu: Indian Clay Tandoor & Curries, Continental Grills & Italian Antipasti, and Indo-Chinese Wok Specialities. We have an extensive range of pure vegetarian dishes prepared in dedicated kitchen sections, including Greek Spanakopita, Tomato & Basil Bruschetta, Paneer Tikka Lababdar, wood-stone pizzas, and artisan mocktails.

We love hosting celebrations! We offer tailored multi-course party packages starting from ₹650 per guest, custom balloon arch decor against our wooden slat wall, cake cutting setups, and dedicated host service. You can use our interactive Party Budget Calculator or call our event coordinator directly at +91 7099018429.

You are warmly welcome to bring your own celebration cake! We provide complimentary refrigeration, plates, cutlery, candles, and cake-stand setup. Alternatively, you can pre-order one of our 1kg handcrafted celebration cakes (Belgian chocolate truffle or red velvet) when booking your party package.

Palate Story is officially listed on Zomato for home delivery across Guwahati. In addition, you can use our Online Ordering page to customize a takeaway order or pre-order your dine-in meal, which dispatches directly to our kitchen via WhatsApp (+91 7099018429).
